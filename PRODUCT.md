# PRODUCT.md — what is being designed

Short, factual context for the design skills. Update it when the product changes.

**Product:** EdithLab / RitaJet. A language-learning site where the learner talks to **Rita**, a
voice teacher (live conversation and curriculum lessons), and studies with flashcards, a German
Lab and a words list built from what they asked Rita.

**Learners:** mostly Arabic speakers (Jordanian dialect) learning English and German. Arabic
and English text sit side by side, so right-to-left and left-to-right both matter.

**Main jobs on screen:** start a call with Rita and speak; read what was said and tap a word
for its meaning and pronunciation; follow a lesson; keep the words they asked about and turn
them into flashcards.

**Mode:** mostly Operate (the learner is doing a task) with a Persuade home page.

**Feel:** calm, friendly, clear; one pink action colour on white with ink text; Rita provides
the personality. Not a generic SaaS look.

**Constraints:** the visual system is in `DESIGN.md`. Performance on phones matters. Never
claim something was saved or understood unless it was.
